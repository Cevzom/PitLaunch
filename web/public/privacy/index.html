Privacy

Privacy Policy

The short version: PitLaunch runs on your PC and stays on your PC. Nothing below changes that — it just says so precisely.

The short version

The installed PitLaunch app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. Every setup you create — displays, audio, window positions, applications — is saved in a file on your own PC. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking of any kind.

What's stored, and where

Your setups live locally, in %APPDATA%\PitLaunch, on the PC PitLaunch is installed on. That includes:

  • Display layouts — which monitors, resolution, refresh rate, and arrangement
  • Audio device preferences
  • Window positions
  • Configured application lists

None of it leaves your machine. PitLaunch has no server to send it to.

No telemetry in the app

The installed PitLaunch app does not phone home to report how you use it. No usage analytics, no crash reporting to a third party, no tracking identifiers. What you do with the app is not PitLaunch's business.

No account required

There is nothing to sign in to. Downloading and running PitLaunch is the entire setup process.

The only network activity

PitLaunch's installed copy checks GitHub in the background for a newer release. That request includes the app version and your OS — enough to know whether an update applies — and nothing tied to you personally. If you choose to update, it downloads the changed files from GitHub. That's the entire list of things PitLaunch does over a network.

If paid features are added later

PitLaunch 1.0 has no paid tier. A future paid tier may use an account system or a license-key system; that decision has not been made. This policy will be updated before any paid system launches, including clear details about what data is processed, why it is needed, and which service providers are involved.
